# Subject

90-Day Strategic Growth Recommendations Based on Customer Intelligence Analysis

---

# Situation

ShopStream has successfully grown its customer acquisition efforts and increased marketing investment by approximately **22%** over the analysis period. The company has developed a strong traffic generation capability and continues to attract new users across multiple acquisition channels.

The business also possesses several strategic strengths:

* Large and diverse customer base.
* Strong revenue-generating customer segments.
* Growing product catalog and traffic sources.
* Sufficient data assets to support advanced analytics and experimentation.

However, increased investment has not translated into proportional business growth.

---

# Complication

The GrowthLens analysis identified four critical challenges that are limiting sustainable growth.

## 1. Customer Conversion Is Underperforming

A significant number of users drop out throughout the purchase journey, resulting in lost revenue opportunities and inefficient marketing spending.

---

## 2. Customer Retention Remains Weak

The business is heavily dependent on acquiring new customers instead of retaining existing ones, making growth increasingly expensive.

---

## 3. Acquisition Economics Are Unhealthy

The current **LTV:CAC ratio of approximately 0.66x** is substantially below the recommended benchmark of **3.0x**, indicating that customer acquisition investments are not generating sufficient long-term value.

---

## 4. High-Value Customers Are Not Protected

A relatively small percentage of customers contribute a disproportionate share of revenue, creating significant concentration risk.

---

# Executive Recommendation

The primary challenge facing ShopStream is not a lack of customer demand.

The challenge is the inability to efficiently:

1. Convert customers.
2. Retain customers.
3. Acquire customers profitably.

Future investments should prioritize customer economics and retention before increasing acquisition spending.
