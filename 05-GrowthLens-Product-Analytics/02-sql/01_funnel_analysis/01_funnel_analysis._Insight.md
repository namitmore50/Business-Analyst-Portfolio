## Query 01 – Overall Conversion Funnel

### Key Finding
27,703 users completed a purchase compared to 63,197 users who entered the funnel.

### Business Insight
Only 43.84% of initial visitors ultimately completed an order. The largest drop occurs between purchase intent events and completed orders.

### Recommendation
Investigate checkout completion, payment success rates, order processing issues, and customer abandonment after purchase intent.
## Query 02 – Funnel by Device Type

### Key Finding
Desktop users account for the vast majority of funnel activity and completed purchases.

### Business Insight
Desktop users generated 78,219 purchases compared to 8,508 purchases from Tablet/Other devices. Desktop remains the primary conversion channel for ShopStream.

### Recommendation
Prioritize desktop user experience improvements while continuing to optimize tablet/mobile experiences to expand conversion opportunities.

### Note
Product viewers exceed home visitors because many users enter directly through product pages, search results, advertisements, or external links.
## Query 03 – Product Category Popularity

### Key Finding
Intimates, Jeans, and Tops & Tees receive the highest customer engagement and product page views.

### Business Insight
Customer interest is concentrated in a small number of apparel categories. These categories should receive priority placement in promotions, recommendations, and inventory planning.

### Recommendation
Increase marketing visibility for top-performing categories while investigating why categories such as Clothing Sets, Jumpsuits & Rompers, and Suits attract significantly lower engagement.
## Query 04 – Time To Convert Distribution

### Key Finding
73.22% of customers complete their first purchase within one day of their first recorded interaction.

### Business Insight
The majority of conversions occur almost immediately after customer engagement. Users who do not convert within the first day are significantly less likely to purchase.

### Recommendation
Focus marketing and remarketing efforts within the first 24 hours after acquisition. Trigger email campaigns, push notifications, and promotional offers during this critical conversion window.
## Query 05 – Traffic Source Funnel Quality

### Key Finding
Facebook delivers the highest conversion rate (27.92%), closely followed by Search (27.80%).

### Business Insight
All acquisition channels perform similarly, indicating a balanced marketing mix. Search contributes the largest customer volume while Facebook achieves the strongest conversion efficiency.

### Recommendation
Continue investing in Search for scale while testing increased budget allocation toward Facebook campaigns to evaluate incremental conversion gains.

## Query 06 – New vs Returning Users

### Key Finding
88.06% of customers are new purchasers, while only 11.94% are returning customers.

### Business Insight
The business is highly dependent on acquiring new customers. Returning customers represent a small segment but generate more purchases per user.

### Recommendation
Implement retention campaigns, loyalty programs, and personalized recommendations to increase repeat purchase behavior and customer lifetime value.

## Query 07 – Peak Conversion Timing Analysis

### Key Finding
The highest conversion volume occurs on Monday at 13:00 with 454 completed orders.

### Business Insight
Order activity is concentrated around Monday and Sunday, with midday hours consistently appearing among the highest-performing conversion periods.

### Recommendation
Schedule promotional emails, push notifications, remarketing campaigns, and flash sales during high-conversion windows, especially Monday afternoons and Sunday daytime periods.
