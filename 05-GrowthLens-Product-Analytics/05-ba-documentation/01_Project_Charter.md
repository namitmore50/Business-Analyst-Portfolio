# 1. Project Overview

## Project Title

GrowthLens – Customer Intelligence & Product Analytics Platform

## Project Sponsor

Chief Product Officer (CPO)

## Business Analyst

Namit More

## Project Type

Business Intelligence and Product Analytics Initiative

## Project Description

GrowthLens is an end-to-end customer intelligence and product analytics platform designed to help ShopStream understand customer behavior, improve retention, optimize marketing investments, and drive revenue growth through data-driven decision-making.

The project combines:

* SQL-based data engineering
* Python analytics and machine learning
* Power BI dashboards
* Business analysis documentation
* Executive recommendations and action planning

---

# 2. Business Justification

ShopStream is experiencing several growth challenges:

* Declining customer retention rates.
* Increasing customer acquisition costs.
* High cart abandonment rates.
* Inefficient marketing channel investments.
* Lack of customer segmentation and personalization.
* Absence of experimentation and decision-support systems.

Without a centralized analytics platform, business stakeholders cannot effectively identify revenue opportunities or prioritize growth initiatives.

GrowthLens addresses these problems by providing actionable insights, predictive analytics, and executive dashboards that support strategic decision-making.

4. Develop customer segmentation models for targeted interventions.
