## Query 32: Weekly Active Buyers Trend

### Key Finding
Weekly active buyers increased from a single buyer in early 2019 to a peak of 891 buyers in January 2024.

### Business Insight
The platform experienced consistent growth over five years, with the strongest acceleration occurring during 2023. Revenue growth closely tracked buyer growth, indicating healthy customer acquisition and engagement.

### Recommendation
Continue monitoring Weekly Active Buyers as the primary North Star metric. Focus on sustaining acquisition efficiency and retention to maintain growth momentum.
## Query 33: Growth Accounting

### Key Finding
January 2024 recorded 1,891 new users and 146 resurrected users, the highest monthly growth observed.

### Business Insight
User growth is primarily driven by new customer acquisition, while the growing number of resurrected users indicates improving re-engagement effectiveness.

### Recommendation
Maintain acquisition momentum while investing in retention programs to increase the proportion of retained users month-over-month.
## Query 34: Top Products by Revenue & YoY Growth

### Key Finding
The North Face Apex Bionic Soft Shell Jacket generated the highest product revenue in 2023 at $2,709.

### Business Insight
Premium outerwear and formalwear categories dominate top-product rankings. Many top-performing products generated little or no revenue in 2022, indicating successful product assortment expansion.

### Recommendation
Increase inventory, promotions, and merchandising support for high-growth outerwear and premium apparel categories while identifying similar products for expansion.
## Query 35: Executive KPI Scorecard

### Key Finding
The business generated $2.72M revenue from 27,703 customers and maintained an LTV:CAC ratio of 2.97.

### Business Insight
Customer acquisition is performing well, but repeat customer rate remains relatively low at 11.94%. Increasing retention and repeat purchases would significantly improve long-term profitability.

### Recommendation
Focus on retention programs, loyalty initiatives, and customer re-engagement campaigns to increase repeat purchase behavior and customer lifetime value.
