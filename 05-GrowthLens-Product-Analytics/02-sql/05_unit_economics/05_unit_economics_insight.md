## Query 27: LTV by Acquisition Channel

### Key Finding
Facebook customers generated the highest 365-day LTV ($16.09), followed by Display ($15.45) and Search ($14.50).

### Business Insight
Search remains the largest revenue contributor because of user volume, while Facebook produces the most valuable customers on a per-user basis.

### Recommendation
Increase investment in channels producing higher LTV customers while maintaining Search as the primary acquisition engine.
## Query 28: CAC by Channel

### Key Finding
Facebook has the highest CAC ($8.60), while Organic has the lowest CAC ($0.66).

### Business Insight
Customer acquisition efficiency varies significantly across channels. Although Facebook acquires higher-value users, it also requires substantially higher acquisition spend.

### Recommendation
Evaluate channels using both CAC and LTV metrics to optimize marketing allocation decisions.
## Query 29: LTV:CAC Ratio by Channel

### Key Finding
Organic achieved the highest LTV:CAC ratio (20.85), followed by Email (4.50).

### Business Insight
Organic and Email channels create highly profitable customer acquisition. Facebook and Display generate valuable customers but acquisition costs limit overall efficiency.

### Recommendation
Increase investment in Organic and Email growth strategies while reducing CAC for Facebook and Display campaigns.

## Query 30: CAC Trend Analysis

### Key Finding
Customer acquisition costs decreased significantly across all acquisition channels between August 2023 and January 2024.

### Business Insight
Marketing performance improved over time, with Facebook and Search showing the largest CAC reductions. Lower acquisition costs improve profitability and increase return on marketing investment.

### Recommendation
Continue scaling channels demonstrating declining CAC while maintaining acquisition quality and customer lifetime value.
## Query 31: Dormant User Revenue at Risk

### Key Finding
18,320 customers have been inactive for more than 180 days, representing ₹1.75 million in at-risk revenue.

### Business Insight
Dormant customers account for a substantial portion of historical revenue. Recovering even a small percentage of these users would generate significant incremental revenue at a lower cost than acquiring new customers.

### Recommendation
Implement win-back campaigns, personalized offers, loyalty incentives, and targeted email programs focused on dormant customers.
