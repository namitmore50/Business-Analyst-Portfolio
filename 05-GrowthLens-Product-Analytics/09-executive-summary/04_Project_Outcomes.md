# Executive Overview

GrowthLens was developed as an end-to-end Customer Intelligence and Product Analytics platform to help ShopStream improve business decision-making through analytics, experimentation, and executive reporting.

The project successfully transformed fragmented customer and operational data into actionable insights, enabling the business to identify growth opportunities, improve customer economics, and establish a scalable analytics framework.

---

# Project Objectives

The project was initiated with the following objectives:

1. Improve visibility into customer acquisition and retention.
2. Understand the drivers behind slowing business growth.
3. Create an experimentation and decision-support framework.
4. Establish executive KPI reporting.
5. Develop strategic recommendations for sustainable growth.

# Lessons Learned

1. Analytics projects require strong business context, not just technical implementation.
2. Customer retention often creates more value than increased acquisition spending.
3. Metric governance is essential for stakeholder trust.
4. Experimentation frameworks significantly improve decision quality.
5. Executive communication is as important as analytical accuracy.
