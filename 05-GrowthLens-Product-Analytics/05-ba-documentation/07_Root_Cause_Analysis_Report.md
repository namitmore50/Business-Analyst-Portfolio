# 1. Problem Statement

Despite increasing marketing expenditure by approximately 22% over the analysis period, ShopStream's Monthly Active Users (MAU) remained largely flat and revenue growth failed to accelerate as expected.

The organization is experiencing:

* Rising Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
* Stagnant Monthly Active Users (MAU)
* Declining marketing efficiency
* Increasing revenue pressure

The business requires a structured analysis to identify the underlying causes of these issues and determine corrective actions.

# 3. Five Whys Analysis

---

## Why #1

### Why did MAU remain flat?

Because new customer acquisition failed to offset customer churn.

### Evidence

Retention rates remained below target and churn reduced net active user growth.

---

## Why #2

### Why was customer acquisition inefficient?

Because conversion rates from traffic to purchase remained low.

### Evidence

Large volumes of traffic failed to convert into purchasing customers.

---

## Why #3

### Why were conversion rates low?

Because customers dropped off during product discovery and checkout stages.

### Evidence

Funnel analysis identified significant abandonment in the middle of the purchase journey.

---

## Why #4

### Why was funnel abandonment high?

Because customer experience and targeting were not sufficiently optimized.

### Evidence

High bounce rates and low returning-user engagement indicated friction in the customer journey.

---

## Why #5

### Why was targeting ineffective?

Because marketing investments focused on volume acquisition rather than customer quality and retention.

### Evidence

Marketing spend increased substantially while LTV:CAC ratios remained significantly below target.

# 5. CAC Decomposition Findings

## Objective

Identify the components responsible for rising acquisition costs.

---

## Components Analyzed

* CPM (Cost per Thousand Impressions)
* CTR (Click Through Rate)
* Landing Page Conversion
* Purchase Conversion

---

## Findings

The primary drivers of rising CAC were:

1. Lower conversion efficiency.
2. High funnel abandonment.
3. Declining marketing quality.

---

## Secondary Contributors

* Increased competition for paid traffic.
* Lower quality traffic sources.
* Reduced customer retention.

---

# Key Conclusion

The issue is not insufficient traffic.

The issue is inefficient conversion of acquired traffic into long-term customers.

---

# 6. Root Cause Statement

## Primary Root Cause

ShopStream's growth challenges are primarily driven by an over-reliance on paid acquisition combined with poor conversion efficiency and weak customer retention.

Additional marketing investment is therefore producing diminishing returns.
