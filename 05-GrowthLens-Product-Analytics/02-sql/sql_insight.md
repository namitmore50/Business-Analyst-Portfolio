# Queries 1–12 Insights

## Q1: Funnel Overview

Most users progress from product view to cart, indicating strong purchase intent. Optimization should focus on increasing initial homepage engagement.

## Q2: Device Performance

Desktop users contribute the majority of purchases and traffic. Desktop experience should remain the primary optimization focus.

## Q3: Category Performance

Intimates, Jeans, and Tops & Tees attract the highest product views, making them key traffic-driving categories.

## Q4: Conversion Time Analysis

Over 73% of customers convert within one day, highlighting the importance of immediate remarketing and retargeting.

## Q5: Traffic Source Performance

Search drives the highest volume of conversions while maintaining a strong conversion rate, making it the most scalable acquisition channel.

## Q6: New vs Returning Users

Most customers are first-time buyers, while returning users place more orders per customer, demonstrating higher long-term value.

## Q7: Order Timing Analysis

Purchase activity peaks around mid-day and weekends, suggesting ideal periods for promotions and campaigns.

## Q8: Cohort Retention Matrix

Retention improves significantly for newer cohorts, indicating successful acquisition and onboarding improvements.

## Q9: Cohort LTV Analysis

Recent cohorts generate substantially higher lifetime value than older cohorts, reflecting improved customer quality.

## Q10: D7/D30/D90 Retention

Retention rates consistently improved across cohorts, demonstrating stronger customer engagement over time.

## Q11: Highest LTV Cohort

The January 2024 cohort generated the highest customer value and should be analyzed for acquisition best practices.

## Q12: Repeat Purchase Rate by Category

Intimates create the strongest customer loyalty and repeat purchasing behavior among all categories.
