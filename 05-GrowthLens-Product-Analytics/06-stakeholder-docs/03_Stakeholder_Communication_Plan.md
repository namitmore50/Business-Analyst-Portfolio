# Stakeholder Communication Plan

**Project:** GrowthLens – Customer Intelligence & Product Analytics Platform
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 24 June 2026
**Author:** Namit More
**Status:** Final

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this communication plan is to ensure all project stakeholders receive the right information at the right time and through the appropriate communication channels.

The communication framework promotes transparency, stakeholder alignment, and timely decision-making throughout the GrowthLens project lifecycle.

---

# Communication Objectives

* Maintain stakeholder engagement.
* Provide timely project updates.
* Escalate issues and risks effectively.
* Support informed decision-making.
* Ensure alignment on business priorities.

# Communication Principles

1. Communicate early and transparently.
2. Use data to support decisions.
3. Escalate risks promptly.
4. Tailor communication to stakeholder needs.
5. Maintain a single source of truth.

# Success Measures

Communication effectiveness will be measured by:

* Stakeholder participation rate.
* Timely decision-making.
* Number of unresolved issues.
* User adoption of dashboards.
* Stakeholder satisfaction.

# Conclusion

The communication plan ensures that all stakeholders remain informed, engaged, and aligned throughout the GrowthLens project lifecycle. Effective communication is essential for transforming analytical insights into business actions and measurable outcomes.
