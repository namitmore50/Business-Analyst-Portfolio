# Executive Overview

GrowthLens is an end-to-end Customer Intelligence and Product Analytics platform developed to help ShopStream make data-driven decisions regarding customer acquisition, retention, experimentation, and revenue optimization.

The project integrates SQL, Python analytics, and Power BI into a single decision-support ecosystem capable of converting raw customer data into actionable business insights and strategic recommendations.

The primary objective of GrowthLens was to identify the reasons behind slowing growth despite increased marketing investments and to recommend initiatives capable of improving profitability and long-term customer value.

---

# Business Context

The e-commerce industry continues to become increasingly competitive. Customer acquisition costs are rising while customer expectations for personalized experiences and seamless purchasing journeys continue to increase.

ShopStream faced several business challenges:

* Increasing marketing expenditure.
* Flat Monthly Active Users (MAU).
* High cart abandonment.
* Weak customer retention.
* Lack of experimentation capabilities.
* Limited visibility into unit economics.

Without a centralized analytics platform, business teams were unable to quickly identify revenue opportunities or understand the drivers behind customer behavior.

---

The GrowthLens platform was designed to achieve the following objectives:

# Key Findings

## Finding 1 – Marketing Spend Increased but Growth Stagnated

Marketing expenditure increased by approximately **22%**, while Monthly Active Users remained largely unchanged.

### Business Impact

Increasing investment alone is not producing sustainable growth.

---

## Finding 2 – Customer Conversion Is Underperforming

Significant customer drop-offs were observed throughout the purchase funnel, particularly during checkout.

### Business Impact

Revenue opportunities are being lost due to conversion inefficiencies.

---

## Finding 3 – Customer Retention Is Weak

The business relies heavily on acquiring new customers rather than retaining existing ones.

### Business Impact

Growth is expensive and difficult to sustain.

---

## Finding 4 – Acquisition Economics Are Unhealthy

The current **LTV:CAC ratio of approximately 0.66x** is significantly below the recommended benchmark of **3.0x**.

### Business Impact

Marketing investments are not generating sufficient long-term value.

---

## Finding 5 – Revenue Concentration Exists

A relatively small percentage of customers generate a disproportionate share of revenue.

### Business Impact

Protecting and growing high-value segments can significantly improve profitability.

# Executive Recommendation

The primary challenge facing ShopStream is not insufficient customer acquisition.

The challenge is the inability to efficiently convert and retain acquired customers.

Future investments should prioritize:

1. Conversion Optimization
2. Customer Retention
3. Acquisition Quality
4. Customer Lifetime Value

before increasing marketing expenditure.
