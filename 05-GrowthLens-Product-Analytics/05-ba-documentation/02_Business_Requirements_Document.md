# 1. Executive Summary

ShopStream operates in a highly competitive e-commerce environment where customer acquisition costs continue to rise while retention and conversion rates remain below industry benchmarks. Business teams currently rely on fragmented reports and manual analysis, limiting their ability to identify revenue opportunities, understand customer behavior, and prioritize strategic initiatives.

GrowthLens is an integrated Customer Intelligence and Product Analytics Platform designed to provide executive visibility into customer journeys, retention, segmentation, experimentation, and unit economics. The platform enables stakeholders to make data-driven decisions and identify opportunities to improve revenue, profitability, and customer engagement.

---

# 2. Business Context

## Industry

E-Commerce and Digital Retail

## Company Profile

ShopStream is a mid-sized online retail platform offering products across multiple categories and serving customers through web and mobile channels.

## Business Challenges

* Increasing customer acquisition costs.
* High cart abandonment.
* Declining customer retention.
* Limited customer segmentation.
* Lack of experimentation framework.
* Insufficient visibility into unit economics.

## Competitive Pressure

Competitors are increasingly using customer analytics, personalization, and experimentation capabilities to improve conversion and retention. ShopStream requires a centralized analytics platform to remain competitive.

# 9. Business Rules

| ID     | Rule                                                 |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| BRL001 | Revenue must be reported in INR.                     |
| BRL002 | Retention is measured monthly.                       |
| BRL003 | Conversion Rate = Orders / Sessions.                 |
| BRL004 | LTV:CAC Ratio = LTV / CAC.                           |
| BRL005 | Gross Profit = Revenue – Cost.                       |
| BRL006 | Cohorts are defined by acquisition month.            |
| BRL007 | Customer segments are generated using RFM analysis.  |
| BRL008 | A/B significance threshold = 95%.                    |
| BRL009 | Forecast scenarios cover six months.                 |
| BRL010 | All dashboards refresh from the analytical database. |

# 11. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if:

1. Seven executive dashboards are delivered.
2. Twenty-five business requirements are implemented.
3. Customer analytics and segmentation capabilities are available.
4. Strategic recommendations identify at least ₹4.8 Cr in business value.
5. Stakeholders can independently use dashboards for decision-making.
6. A complete business documentation package is delivered.
7. A 90-day growth roadmap is produced with measurable KPIs.
