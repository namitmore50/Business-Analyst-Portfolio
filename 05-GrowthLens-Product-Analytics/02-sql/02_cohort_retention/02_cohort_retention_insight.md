## Query 08: Monthly Cohort Retention Matrix

### Business Question
How many users from each acquisition cohort return and purchase in subsequent months?

### Key Findings
- Monthly acquisition cohorts grew significantly from 2019 to 2024.
- Early cohorts (2019–2020) had very small sizes, while 2023 cohorts exceeded 1,000 users.
- Most retention occurs in Month 0, indicating users typically make only one purchase.
- Retention drops sharply after the first month across almost all cohorts.
- Only a small number of customers remain active after 6–12+ months.

### Business Insight
Customer acquisition is growing strongly, but long-term retention remains low. Growth is primarily driven by new customer acquisition rather than repeat purchasing, highlighting an opportunity to improve loyalty and retention programs.
## Query 09 – Cumulative LTV by Acquisition Cohort

### Key Finding
The Jan 2024 cohort generated the highest lifetime value, producing ₹91,291.53 in cumulative revenue.

### Business Insight
Customer acquisition quality has improved significantly over time. Recent cohorts generate substantially more revenue than earlier cohorts, indicating stronger acquisition targeting and customer monetization.

### Recommendation
Analyze acquisition channels, campaigns, and product mix associated with high-performing 2023–2024 cohorts and replicate these strategies across future marketing efforts.
## Query 10 – D7, D30, D90 Retention Rates

### Key Finding
Recent cohorts demonstrate substantially stronger retention performance than historical cohorts.

### Business Insight
D90 retention increased from approximately 1–3% in early cohorts to more than 25% in late 2023 and early 2024 cohorts. This indicates meaningful improvements in customer engagement and repeat purchasing behavior.

### Recommendation
Analyze acquisition channels, campaigns, onboarding flows, and product experiences associated with high-retention cohorts and scale these successful practices across future customer acquisition efforts.

## Query 11 – Highest LTV Cohort Analysis

### Key Finding
The January 2024 cohort generated the highest lifetime value and was dominated by customers acquired through Search.

### Business Insight
More than 70% of users in the highest-value cohort originated from Search traffic. The cohort exhibited a balanced gender distribution and an average age around 41 years, suggesting broad demographic appeal.

### Recommendation
Increase investment in Search acquisition campaigns and analyze the keywords, landing pages, and campaigns responsible for attracting these high-value customers.

## Query 12 – Repeat Purchase Rate by Product Category

### Key Finding
Intimates achieved the highest repeat purchase rate at 10.02%, nearly double most other categories.

### Business Insight
Customers are more likely to repurchase essential apparel items such as Intimates, Underwear, Socks, and Pants. These categories create stronger long-term customer relationships and recurring revenue streams.

### Recommendation
Develop subscription programs, loyalty campaigns, and personalized recommendations for high-repeat categories to maximize customer lifetime value.

## Query 13 – Churn Inflection Point

### Key Finding
Users with only one session experience a churn rate of 74.67%, while churn drops below 50% once users reach five sessions.

### Business Insight
The most important engagement milestone occurs around the fifth session. Users who reach this threshold demonstrate significantly stronger retention and conversion behavior.

### Recommendation
Design onboarding flows, remarketing campaigns, and engagement initiatives focused on moving users from their first session to at least five sessions as quickly as possible.
## Query 14 – Loyalty Revenue Contribution

### Key Finding
Customers acquired more than six months ago generate 88.86% of total revenue.

### Business Insight
The business relies heavily on long-term customer relationships. Existing customers contribute nearly nine times more revenue than recently acquired customers.

### Recommendation
Increase investment in customer retention, loyalty programs, personalized marketing, and repeat purchase initiatives to maximize customer lifetime value.
